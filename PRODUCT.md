## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML/CSS/JS (confirmed binding): `index.html`, `css/style.css`, `js/script.js`. No framework or build step.

## Users

A mixed public audience; one page must serve all of them:
- Hiring managers and recruiters, including international employers, assessing a senior AI and full-stack engineer.
- Freelance and consulting clients deciding whether to hire for AI, full-stack or automation delivery.
- Students, community members, followers, peers and network contacts (IEEE, ACM, GDG, SMEC, NASA Space Apps circles) looking at the work and the person.

## Product Purpose

Personal portfolio of Mohammad Nabeel Sohail, a Senior AI & Full-Stack Software Engineer and AI Team Lead. It presents his experience, production AI work, projects, leadership and community roles, and gives visitors a route to contact him or download his CV. Success means each visitor type finds what they came for: a hire, an engagement, or a connection.

## Positioning

Production-scale AI plus full-stack ownership, from research to deployment and monitoring. He leads an AI team at FTech Solutions (Dawat-e-Islami), serving millions of users globally. He also runs a freelance consultancy and has years of community leadership. The visitors-believe-one-thing question was answered "all", because different visitors need different proof. No single claim is ranked above the rest.

## Operating Context

Now based in Middlesbrough, UK (as of Oct 2026); British national born in the UK, raised in Kuwait, studied and worked in Karachi. The site should read as global, not tied to one country. Content lives in `content.md` (profile, experience, skills) and `projects/*.md` (aegiss, am-violation, disano, dominica, eventbridgeai, nehohouse, pyranex, seatswap, snipeitnow, speechpro, zetpay). `NabeelSohail-CV.pdf` is the downloadable CV. `AUDIT.md` and `Task.md` hold an earlier UX/UI audit backlog (nav gaps, CTA count, reduced motion, scroll offset).

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Static site; no backend.
- Existing sections include hero, about, experience timeline, leadership cards, stack, projects and contact.
- Primary actions in the hero: view work and download CV. The earlier audit recommended two CTAs, not three.
- Open decision: which single audience or message leads the first viewport is undecided.

## Brand Commitments

Name: Mohammad Nabeel Sohail (goes by Nabeel). Voice in current content is first-person, warm and purposeful: "quality over quantity", "AI infrastructure that holds under real-world pressure". Existing assets: `profile.jpg`, `favicon.svg`, `og-image.png`, CV PDF.

## Evidence on Hand

Real roles and dates in `content.md`, 11 project write-ups in `projects/`, CV PDF, profile photo, OG image. No testimonials, client logos or quantified benchmarks are confirmed; future work must not invent them.

## Product Principles

1. Serve several audiences without making any of them dig; route visitors by intent.
2. Proof over claims: lead with real projects, roles and scale already documented.
3. Personal and credible, not generic: the person (leader, builder, community-minded) is part of the product.
4. Stay static and fast; no dependency or build complexity.
5. Never fabricate evidence.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

No product-specific standard stated. The audit flagged a missing `prefers-reduced-motion` check; treat respectful motion and sound contrast as baseline.
